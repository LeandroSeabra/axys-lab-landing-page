Geophysical instruments

A resistivity meter and drone sensors, in development in Belém, Brazil, to measure the subsurface where the forest will not let you walk.

See the instruments Simulate a flight

We design the instruments the Axys Geo Soluções crews take to the field.

Axys Geo Soluções runs geophysical surveys through Axys Geofísica and monitors dams through Axys Safe, across the Amazon. The Lab grew out of that routine: we build instruments for the region's heat, humidity, forest and logistics, tested by the same crews who will use them.

Three instruments

All three are in development. Each is shown over the kind of data it produces; drawings and data are illustrative, and prototype photos will follow once field tests are complete.

Resistivity meter

Injects current through one pair of electrodes and measures the potential difference at others, building electrical resistivity sections of the subsurface.

Method
Electrical resistivity
Measures
Apparent resistivity of the subsurface
Platform
Ground, with electrodes
Typical use
Groundwater, dams, contamination, shallow geology

Drone magnetometer

A sensor towed below the drone, away from motor interference, recording variations in the Earth's magnetic field along the flight lines.

Method
Magnetics
Measures
Total magnetic field intensity
Platform
Multirotor drone
Typical use
Mineral exploration, structural mapping, shallow targets

Drone gamma-ray spectrometer

A detector that counts the gamma rays naturally emitted by rocks and soils and separates the potassium, thorium and uranium contributions.

Method

Measures
Natural radioactivity: K, eTh and eU
Platform
Multirotor drone
Typical use
Geological mapping, hydrothermal alteration, soils

Higher means weaker

The signal of a magnetic body fades fast with distance. That is why the drone flies low, just above the canopy, where an aircraft cannot go. Drag and watch the reading change.

In northern Brazil the Earth's field is almost horizontal, so a body shows up as a low flanked by two highs rather than as a peak. Simulation of a sphere 40 m deep, with −5° inclination. Illustrative values.

From field to bench

First, in the field. Every project starts on an Axys Geo Soluções survey: what has to be measured, how deep, on what terrain, in what heat and with what access. That becomes the instrument's requirements list.

Then, on the bench in Belém. Electronics, firmware, housing and calibration are developed in the Lab. Each version is tested against known responses, like these simulated magnetic profiles, before it leaves the bench.

Finally, back to the field. The prototype flies and measures on real Axys Geofísica surveys, alongside established equipment. Only after that is it ready for other clients.

Where to measure

  • Outlining iron, copper, gold and other targets in provinces such as Carajás, with low flights and tight line spacing.

  • Separating lithologies, tracing faults and contacts, and mapping hydrothermal alteration under soil and vegetation.

  • Locating aquifers, estimating sediment thickness and choosing where to drill wells.

  • Investigating foundations, seepage zones and moisture in dam bodies, an area where Axys already works in monitoring.

  • Mapping contamination plumes, waste disposal areas and disturbed soils.

  • Instruments for universities and institutes working in applied geophysics in the Amazon.

Questions

When will the instruments be ready?

All three are in development and being field-tested with the Axys Geofísica crews. Registered contacts receive the schedule first.

Will you sell or rent them?

Commercial terms will be presented first to registered contacts and pilot participants.

Do the sensors work with any drone?

They are being designed for heavy-lift survey multirotors. Compatibility by model will be listed in the specifications.

Will there be maintenance and calibration?

Yes. Maintenance, calibration and technical support are planned in Belém, close to the people working in the Amazon.

Can I order a custom instrument?

Talk to us: the Lab was born to solve field problems, and custom projects are assessed case by case.
