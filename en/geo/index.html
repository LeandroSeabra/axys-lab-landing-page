Your target

Each method sees a different property of the subsurface. Pick what you are looking for and see which one responds.

Electrical resistivity

Strong response

Water makes the ground more conductive: resistivity drops where it is.

Magnetics

No response

Water is not magnetic; the field does not change.

Gamma-ray spectrometry

Weak response

It reads only the surface; it notices water only if it changes the topsoil.

Typical, simplified responses. The right method depends on depth, contrast and terrain, which is what we define during planning.

From field to report

  1. Planning. The project question sets the method, line spacing and access before the crew mobilises.

  2. Acquisition. In-house crews on the ground or by drone, with data quality control while still in the field.

  3. Processing. Corrections, filtering and inversion to reliable maps and sections.

  4. Interpretation. Geology read from the data, with clear recommendations for decision makers.

Questions

How much does a survey cost?

It depends on the method, line length, access and logistics. Once we understand the area and the project question, we send a fixed-scope proposal with schedule and price.

How long does it take?

Fieldwork usually takes from a few days to a few weeks, depending on the area. The full schedule, including processing and reporting, is in the proposal.

How deep can you see?

It depends on the method and array: resistivity reaches tens of metres; magnetics responds from shallow to deep depending on the source; gamma-ray spectrometry reads the surface. We define this during planning.

Do you work in remote areas?

Yes. We plan logistics for forest, rivers and rain, and fly drones where walking is hard.

What do I receive at the end?

A technical report with method, data quality, maps, sections, interpretation and recommendations, plus raw and processed data in open formats.

What about drone flight permits?

Operations follow Brazilian aviation rules (ANAC and DECEA), and we handle the permits required for each flight.
